Towson University Hackathon 2026

Twelve hours to build something bold.

Organized by Bit Brothers and Sister Scripters, Code/Clash (Code Clash) is a free, 12-hour hackathon for Towson University (TU) students in Towson, Maryland. Come together to explore coding, learn new skills, and turn ambitious ideas into working projects. Build alongside mentors and fellow creators, sharpen your skills, and take a project from first sketch to final demo.

Whether you are a seasoned hacker or opening your editor for the first time, this beginner-friendly student hackathon has a role and a team for you. Join us November 14, 2026, on the Towson University campus.

“Wait—do I need to be an expert?”

Nope! Beginners belong here, too.

Questions from the crowd

Hackathon FAQs.

What is Code Clash?

Code Clash is a 12-hour student hackathon at Towson University. Work with a team to turn an idea into a working project, with workshops and mentors to help along the way.

When and where is the hackathon?

Code Clash takes place November 14, 2026, on the Towson University campus in Towson, Maryland.

Is Code Clash free?

Yes. Entry to the Code Clash hackathon is free.

Do I need a team?

No. Come solo and we will help you meet collaborators.

Do I need coding experience?

No. This beginner-friendly hackathon welcomes first-time participants as well as experienced coders. Workshops and mentors will help you get moving.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, student ID, and your best ideas.
