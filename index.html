The origin story

Twenty-four hours to build something bold.

Code/Clash is a 24-hour hackathon where Towson students come together to explore, learn, and turn ambitious ideas into working projects. Build alongside mentors and fellow creators, sharpen your skills, and take a project from first sketch to final demo.

Whether you are a seasoned hacker or opening your editor for the first time, there is a role and a team for you. Join us November 13–14, 2026, on the Towson University campus.

“Wait—do I need to be an expert?”

Nope! Beginners belong here, too.
